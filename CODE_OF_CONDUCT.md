# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Participants must communicate professionally, respect differing viewpoints, and
avoid personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, or unwanted contact.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation
when conduct falls below these standards. To report a concern privately, contact
[support@sealedcash.com](mailto:support@sealedcash.com).
